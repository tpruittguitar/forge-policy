LOCATION_METHOD=Tim's geo map score for the job's site (the scoring model's geo points; Tim edits them on the map).

- PAY WORTH (Tim 2026-10-19 02:14 / 02:17 ET; updated Tim 2026-10-28): pay is scored, not hard-floored. PAY_WORTH (0-100) compares 75% of the base-pay range (low + 0.75*(high-low), slightly higher than the midpoint) with what the place needs on Tim's geo map: NEED = $300,000 - $120,000 x geo score / 100 ($180,000 at geo 100, $300,000 at geo 0). A remote role is scored at home. PAY_WORTH = 100 / (1 + e^-((pay / NEED - 0.8525) / 0.085)), a smooth curve: pay at 70% of NEED = 14, 80% = 35, 90% = 64, 100% = 85, 120% = 98.

- BASE PAY FLOOR (Tim 2026-10-23): a role whose base pay tops out under $190,000 a year is declined. Test the top of the range (SALARY_BASE_HIGH, else the single posted or estimated value), never the midpoint. Posted pay and FORGE estimates both count (estimates are real pay; see COMPENSATION_ESTIMATION). Hourly pay is annualized at 2,080 hours. Base only: bonus, equity and package adders do not lift a role over the floor. No pay at all is not a decline. The floor applies to active rows only; APPLIED, REJECTED_BY_EMPLOYER, DECLINED_BY_TIM, ANTI_RESURRECTION=YES, DO_NOT_REWORK, rows awaiting Tim (TIM_DECISION_REQUIRED, BLOCKED) and rows carrying a Tim ruling, override or note are never auto-declined. Decline: DECLINED_BY_TIM, DECLINE_REASON_CODE=PAY_BELOW_FLOOR, DECLINE_REASON_TEXT stating the pay evidence and its basis; reopen when newer posted or estimated base reaches $190,000. The floor sits beside PAY_WORTH: roles at or over it are still scored by PAY_WORTH as below.

- PAY_WORTH is the compensation part of the job score. Pay status: PASS at 50 or more; PAY HOLD only under 15 when the role has no remote or relocation upside; otherwise CHECK and the job keeps moving.

- Several plausible worksites: scored at the best site; sites that disagree on PASS / CHECK / HOLD are CHECK. A role outside the US is CHECK.

- Tim tunes PAY_WORTH by changing geo map scores; the NEED range and cutoffs are settings in the scoring model.

- Posted target-role base range is preferred. For PAY_WORTH, use 75% of the range (low + 0.75*(high-low)), not the midpoint (Tim 2026-10-28).

- Base pay is what PAY_WORTH scores. Bonus, equity, vehicle programs, and other package adders do not raise PAY_WORTH. Relocation support only removes a PAY HOLD (the role stays CHECK).

- Closer to home is a preference, not an automatic veto or artificial score bonus.

- Manager/Senior Manager may remain viable when compensation and scope meet the applicable rule; title alone does not kill a role.

- Pure first-line/group-leader/individual-contributor roles normally fall below target unless there is a documented leadership pathway and economics still work.

SECTION=COMPENSATION_ESTIMATION

- Perfect pay data is not required. Transparent uncertainty is required.

- SALARY RULE (Tim 2026-10-19 01:34 ET): an estimated salary is a real salary. Show it and use it everywhere: popup, table, drawer, scoring and decisions. Never treat it as PAY UNKNOWN, and never hold or stop a job because its pay is estimated. PAY UNKNOWN only when there is no posted pay and no estimate.

- An estimate is real pay for display, scoring, PAY_WORTH and decisions. Hold on pay only when PAY_WORTH is a PAY HOLD. Confidence lowers a label only. PAY UNKNOWN only with no posted pay and no estimate.

- Source effort order: target employer/posting; employer-authored comparable role; credible pay-transparency/employer comparison; comparable company/title/scope/geography; role-specific recruiter/job-board claim; weaker market data only as sanity check. When none of these yield a numeric anchor, BLS OEWS (or equivalent) is required as the last step (see REQUIRED UNANCHORED ESTIMATE).

- Exact employer-posted target-role pay is VERIFIED, not an estimate.

- One relevant numeric employer-posted comparable may support a provisional LOW-confidence estimate.

- Two relevant numeric comparables, including at least one strong employer-posted band, may support a qualified estimate; LOW by default, MED when the role/geo match is close and corroboration is strong.

- Three or more strong independent relevant anchors may support higher confidence under the same evidence logic.

- REQUIRED UNANCHORED ESTIMATE (Tim 2026-10-28): if no clean numeric anchor exists, an ESTIMATED / LOW value is still required so the row can be scored and processed. Produce a RANGE that spans (a) BLS OEWS state wages for the role's occupation (P25-P90) and (b) any available text-based model point estimates (JobPredictor, nginyc, or another). Range low = min of BLS P25 and all model points; range high = max of BLS P90 and all model points. If no model points are available, the range is BLS P25-P90 and the basis states that. Prefer posted pay or anchored estimates when they exist. The value used for PAY_WORTH is 75% of the resulting range (low + 0.75*(high-low)). Confidence = LOW. An unanchored estimate is real pay for display, scoring, PAY_WORTH and the $190k floor.

- Aggregator-only or unsupported legacy numbers do not become verified compensation and must not be recycled as posted fact. They stay usable as ESTIMATED pay (basis and confidence recorded).

- Every estimate used for a disposition records: ESTIMATED flag, range/value, HIGH/MED/LOW confidence, sources attempted, evidence/anchors, key uncertainty, whether uncertainty could change the disposition, governing rule, AS-OF date, and verification note. Compensation estimates expire/review after approximately 30 days unless replaced by verified evidence. Uncertainty or age never moves a row to MANUAL_RESEARCH or HOLD; only a PAY_WORTH PAY HOLD does.

- A later verified value immediately supersedes an estimate.

- Missing pay means no posted pay and no estimate. Missing pay must not hide a real role or block the daily brief indefinitely. If decision-changing, keep it MANUAL_RESEARCH/VERIFY COMP. If decision-irrelevant, mark VERIFY_LATER and proceed.

- Never estimate protected facts: exact requisition identity, APPLIED, REJECTED_BY_EMPLOYER, WITHDRAWN, CLOSED/DEAD, citizenship, security-clearance status, legal/work authorization, or whether a specific application was submitted.

- If multiple plausible identities/worksites exist, do not average or invent them. If all plausible cases lead to the same disposition, the role may be processed while the identity/location remains VERIFY_LATER.
